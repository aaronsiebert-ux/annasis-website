Privacy

Privacy & Cookie Notice

This notice explains what the AnnaSIS website (www.annasis.com) collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers this marketing website only, not the AnnaSIS product.

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Who we are

AnnaSIS operates this website. Questions about this notice or your information: sales@annasis.com, or use the Contact page.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you book a meeting or send a request (for example on the Contact page or through Anna chat), we collect what you enter: name, email, organization, phone, and the details or questions you share.
  • Anna chat. Anna is a scripted assistant on our site, not a live person. Your chat history, the topics and questions you choose, and any answers you give are kept in your browser for the current tab session. They are only sent to us if you choose to submit a request.
  • Visitor information. Like most websites, we receive technical details such as your IP address, browser, pages viewed and referring site. Before our visitor-identification tool loads, your browser looks up the organization that owns your network (for example a school district, business or internet provider) so we can skip obvious non-customer traffic such as data centers.
  • Skipped-visitor log. When that check skips a visitor, we keep a short log entry: the network organization name, the reason category, the page path and the time. No IP address is stored in this log, and it never goes to our CRM.

Our own scripts use your browser’s session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab. Third-party tools we use may set their own cookies; their names and durations are set by those providers.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not currently use a separate web analytics service.

How we use information

  • To respond to your requests, schedule meetings and follow up.
  • To understand which organizations visit our site, so our team can reach the right schools and organizations.
  • To filter out bots and infrastructure traffic.
  • To run, secure and improve the website.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the website and respond to you:

  • Apollo.io — website visitor identification.
  • Pipedrive — our CRM; it receives meeting and contact requests, and provides the scheduler, web form and chat loader.
  • Cloudflare — runs the small service that passes your requests to Pipedrive and stores the skipped-visitor log.
  • ipwho.is — the network-owner lookup your browser makes before the visitor tool loads.
  • Google Fonts — serves the fonts used on this site.
  • Our hosting providers — host and deliver the website.

Retention

Session storage is cleared when you close the tab. Skipped-visitor log entries are deleted automatically after 90 days. Requests you send us are kept in our CRM for as long as needed to work with you, or until you ask us to delete them. Third-party providers keep data according to their own policies.

Your choices

  • You can block or clear cookies and site data in your browser settings; the site still works without them.
  • You can use a browser or extension that blocks tracking scripts.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email sales@annasis.com.

Students and children

This website is meant for school, organization and business staff and is not intended for students or children. Information that schools and other customers manage in the AnnaSIS product is handled under their agreements with us, not this notice.

Changes

We may update this notice as the website changes. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.
