School integrations

OneRoster-first migration for private schools.

ANNASIS can ingest OneRoster roster data — directly from a SIS that speaks OneRoster, or through an aggregator such as Clever or ClassLink that normalizes the export. Most student, staff, class, and enrollment data travels on that standard path; anything outside OneRoster is handled with assisted migration. Clever and ClassLink are optional paid services at many schools — we do not assume every campus already has them.

This page lists school software packages commonly in private-education conversations. Logos and names identify packages that support OneRoster export or that are often reached through a OneRoster-compatible rostering hub. They are not partnership, certification, or endorsement badges.

Via OneRoster aggregators

Clever and ClassLink as rostering hubs.

When a school already uses a rostering aggregator, ANNASIS can ingest OneRoster-compatible data through that hub. Aggregators normalize roster feeds from many SIS packages — including schools that do not expose a native OneRoster API to every third party. They are typically optional, paid services; do not assume every private school has Clever or ClassLink.

Clever

Rostering hub that supports OneRoster CSV/SFTP and connects many SIS packages. Optional paid service at the school.

ClassLink

Roster Server shares OneRoster® data (API or CSV) from the school’s SIS. Optional identity/rostering stack — confirm campus licensing.
