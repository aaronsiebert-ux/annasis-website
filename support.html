How can we help?

Three paths

Parents / families

For grades, balances, schedules, or enrollment status, contact your school office first — they own the Student and family record.

If something in ANNASIS itself is broken (for example, you cannot sign in), use Get support in chat with Anna or the support form below.

We use ANNASIS software

Tell us what you need help with. Categories match Anna’s support flow: Login / account access, Billing or payment, Registration or event, Store / order, Gradebook / SIS, or Something else.

The ANNASIS team will follow up by email.
