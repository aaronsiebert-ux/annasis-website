AnnaSIS Network

Shared programs across schools, without merging.

A School System or Operator runs scholarships, events, a school store and shared activities for every member school from one place. Each school keeps its own board, its own records and its own day-to-day. Families get one simple way to take part, right from their own school account.

Are we a fit? Talk with Sales

How it works

One publisher. Independent schools. Families in their own account.

  1. 1

    A School System or Operator publishes

    Set up a shared program once — a scholarship, an event, a store or a season — and choose which member schools take part.

  2. 2

    Member schools stay independent

    Each school keeps its own operations, on AnnaSIS or another OneRoster SIS. Nothing merges; the program simply shows up for their families.

  3. 3

    Families join in their own school account

    Parents apply, register or order from the account they already use, with charges and awards on their own household bill.
