## 1. Competitor feature / messaging themes

### CampBrain
- **Shape:** Full camp management suite — online registration + customer portal; medical management; attendance; staff; meal planning; accommodations/resources; fundraising; financial reconciliation; customizable reporting.
- **Verticals:** School & auxiliary, private overnight, day, faith-based, special needs, YMCA/agency, conference centers.
- **Standouts marketed:** Parent-friendly registration; “Cabinizer” cabin/team assignment; Square POS / camper accounts / inventory; hiring dashboard; forms with upload limits (photos, immunization); attendance (absences, authorize pickups); fundraising landing pages + receipts.
- **Tone:** Delightful, fit-first sales; data → action; support everyone from business office to medical staff.
- **Implication:** Category bar for **registration + medical + cabin ops + staff hiring** is high. ANNASIS should not race “Cabinizer / nurse EHR” on page one — lead where we are stronger (store, fundraising, multi-segment community).

### UltraCamp
- **Shape:** Comprehensive camp management + online registration; multi-program (resident, day, family, retreat/conference).
- **Standouts marketed:** Conditional registration; payment plans; e-sign waivers; automatic waitlists; parent dashboard; **camp store deposits + POS/barcode**; deep **nurse/med** (FDA med list, distribution, Kardex, incident logs); donations/fundraising CRM-ish tools; staff apps + background checks + cabin assignments; photo gallery; group/facility booking.
- **Tone:** “You’ll never need another platform”; partner language; ops + nurse + store + donations breadth.
- **Implication:** UltraCamp already claims **store deposits** and **donations** — ANNASIS still differentiates with an **institutional store** (merch/commerce module) + school/church shared community, not only camper house-account POS.

### CampMinder
- **Shape:** Do-it-all summer-camp ops platform (esp. residential / day).
- **Standouts marketed:** Registration & forms; staff management (refs, apps, payroll-integrated HR); **Health Center**; email/SMS; transportation + attendance; custom reporting; parent app / photo feed; APIs/Zapier.
- **Tone:** Built by camp people; innovation in service of summer; cabin assignment culture.
- **Implication:** Health Center + staff HR + transportation are CampMinder’s fortress — keep ANNASIS medical/staff copy honest (forms / health info / program ops).

### ACTIVE Camp & Class Manager (category context)
- Multi-program registration, waitlists, payments/plans, attendance/check-in, merchandise/inventory, communications — useful for “class + camp” buyers; less overnight-ops depth than CampMinder.
