School management modules

Twelve foundation modules. One student and family record.

These twelve modules are the foundation — not an all-inclusive catalog of every school job. They are the core AnnaSIS is built on so the office can stop relying on separate tools and processes, and Parents, Students, and Faculty stop feeling the gaps. Run them beside another SIS or as the fuller suite. Scope a first site to the modules the office will run this term. The result is a better experience for Parents and Students, streamlined tools and operations for the office, and lower cost for private schools.

Where it matters

Success broken down.

Empower Student success in the classroom and portal. Empower Parent success on the school’s own site and SMS. Empower Faculty success without another login. Empower School success when one foundation covers the operation — not a stack of separate tools.

The foundation modules below are where most private-school operations start. Your campus may run more on top of this base over time.

1. Admissions

Inquiry, online application, enrollment, and re-enrollment — one Admissions pipeline with configurable application forms. Embed Apply on the website you already have.

Enrollment packets can collect waivers and consents on the same student and family record. Families do not leave the school site to start. Staff see one pipeline instead of a form tool plus a spreadsheet plus the SIS.

2. Student Information System (SIS)

Student and family records, demographics, Attendance, Scheduler, Behavior / Discipline, staff roster, and reporting — the system of record. Not a gradebook that later grows a finance surprise.

The family (household) is the unit. Scheduler and Student locator keep the office current on where Students are during the day. Family directory and demographics help you understand your household base. Incident and discipline logs stay on the student record with emergency contacts and roles — ready for Gradebook, fees, and camps. Analytics and dashboards give the office a clear view without exporting to five spreadsheets.

3. Gradebook

Online Gradebook, report cards, progress reports, transcripts, and GPA. Faculty live here; Parents and Students see the same record in the family portal.

No separate parent grade portal. Progress reports and report cards publish from what Faculty already entered — what Parents and Students read is the same academic record.

4. Tuition & billing

Tuition management: payment plans, invoices, incidental billing, online payments, and family-portal pay. Finance is part of the operating system — not a later module.

Incumbents often stay inexpensive until financials are added. AnnaSIS does not reserve tuition & billing for a later SKU.

5. Health & medical records

Immunizations, allergies, medications, and medical fields on the student record — including emergency-card details the office and nurse need. Athletics physicals that expire use the same workflows as eligibility and clearance.

Nurse, coach, and office look at one Student — not three binders and a separate forms tool.

6. School store

Uniforms, books, trips, and fees — school ecommerce on the family record, not a volunteer Shopify the office reconciles at night.

Store purchases land on the family beside tuition & billing. No second shop login to reconcile.

7. Communication

Email and SMS messaging to groups, teams, and lists. Family portal for Parents — no required app.

Notices reach Parents, Students, and Faculty on the student and family record — no required app. Student locator and family demographics sit with the SIS Scheduler.

8. Event registration

Sports, plays, camps, enrichment, extracurricular programs, games, and fundraisers — online registration and payment without add-ons and high fees.

Stop Eventbrite leakage. Event registration embeds on the school site, can collect waivers and consents, and stays on the same student and family record as tuition & billing.

9. Donations & fundraising

One-time and recurring giving, optional round-up, same family wallet.

Development is not a third processor with a third family list.

10. Accounting (QuickBooks)

Accounting integration: the school keeps QuickBooks as books of record. AnnaSIS posts operations.

We do not replace the accountant’s general ledger. We stop the office from typing the same family three times.

11. Uniform exchange

A unique family-portal uniform exchange: Parents publish uniforms for other Parents or Students to exchange or purchase. No middleman required.

Used uniforms stay in the community — not a Facebook thread Faculty and the office cannot see.

12. Athletics eligibility & workflows

Athletics eligibility and clearance with waivers, consents, and serial or parallel approval workflows. Reusable for enrollment packets and purchase approval — without a bolt-on forms product like FinalForms.

Clearance sits on the same student and family record as fees and roster — configured by the school, not another vendor beside your SIS.
