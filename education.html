School operating system

Twelve capabilities. One family record.

Show the suite when the office is tired of stitching a student system, a forms product, a ticketing site, a store, and QuickBooks — and Parents, Students, and Faculty are tired of the seams. Scope a first site to the pillars the office will run this term.

Where it matters

Success broken down.

Empower Student success in the classroom and portal. Empower Parent success on the school’s own site and SMS. Empower Faculty success without another login. Empower School success when the office stops stitching tools.

1. Admissions / enrollment

Inquiry to application to contract to re-enroll. Embed Apply on the website you already have.

Families do not leave the school site to start. Staff see one pipeline instead of a form tool plus a spreadsheet plus the SIS.

2. Student Information System (SIS)

Households, attendance, scheduling, staff roles. System of record — not a gradebook that later grows a finance surprise.

The household is the unit. Siblings, emergency contacts, and roles stay attached when the office opens grades, fees, or a camp.

3. Grades

Gradebook, report cards, transcripts, GPA. Faculty live here; Parents and Students see the same record on the phone.

No separate parent grade portal. What Faculty posts is what Parents and Students read.

4. Tuition

Plans, invoices, incidental fees, family-portal pay. Finance is part of the operating system — not a later module.

Incumbents often stay inexpensive until financials are added. AnnaSIS does not reserve tuition for a later SKU.

5. Health

Immunizations and health fields on the student. Athletics physicals that expire use the same workflow engine as clearance.

Nurse, coach, and office look at one student — not three binders and a separate forms tool.

6. School store

Uniforms, books, trips, and fees — institutional commerce, not a volunteer Shopify the office reconciles at night.

Store purchases land on the household beside tuition. No second shop login to reconcile.

7. Communication

Email + SMS to groups, teams, and lists. Responsive portal for Parents — no required app.

Notices reach Parents, Students, and Faculty on the record. No one is asked to install another application.

8. Events

Sports, plays, camps, enrichment, games, fundraisers — register and pay without add-ons and high fees.

Stop Eventbrite leakage. Registration embeds on the school site and stays on the same family record as tuition.

9. Fundraising + Donations

One-time and recurring give, optional round-up, same family wallet.

Development is not a third processor with a third family list.

10. Accounting + QuickBooks

The school keeps QuickBooks as books of record. AnnaSIS posts operations.

We do not replace the accountant’s system. We stop the office from typing the same family three times.

11. Parent uniform swap

Our unique parent portal lets Parents publish uniforms for other Parents or Students to exchange or purchase. No middleman required.

Used uniforms stay in the community — not a Facebook thread Faculty and the office cannot see.

12. Athletics + Workflows engine

Serial or parallel approvals. Packaged for athletics clearance; reusable for enrollment packets and purchase approval.

Clearance sits on the same family record as fees and roster — configured by the school, not another vendor beside your SIS.
