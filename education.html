School operating system

Twelve capabilities. One family record.

Show the suite when the office is tired of stitching a student system, a forms product, a ticketing site, a store, and QuickBooks. Scope a first site to the pillars the office will run this term. Do not imply every pillar must go live on day one.

1. Admissions / enrollment

Inquiry to application to contract to re-enroll. Embed Apply on the website you already have.

Families do not leave the school site to start. Staff see one pipeline instead of a form tool plus a spreadsheet plus the SIS.

2. Student information

Households, attendance, scheduling, staff roles. System of record — not a gradebook that later grows a finance surprise.

The household is the unit. Siblings, emergency contacts, and roles stay attached when the office opens grades, fees, or a camp.

3. Grades

Gradebook, report cards, transcripts, GPA. Teachers live here; families see the same record on the phone.

No separate parent grade portal. What the teacher posts is what the family reads.

4. Tuition

Plans, invoices, incidental fees, family-portal pay. Finance is part of the operating system — not a later module.

Incumbents often stay inexpensive until financials are added. AnnaSIS does not reserve tuition for a later SKU.

5. Health

Immunizations and health fields on the student. Expiring physicals use the same workflow engine as athletics.

Nurse, coach, and office look at one student — not three binders.

6. School store

Uniforms, books, and fees. Institutional commerce, not a volunteer Shopify.

Store purchases land on the household beside tuition. The office does not reconcile a second shop login.

7. Communication

Email + SMS to groups, teams, and lists. Responsive parent portal. No required app.

Notices go to the people on the record. Families are not asked to install another application.

8. Events

Camps, enrichment, games, fundraisers — register and pay on the school record.

Events are a pillar of the school, not a side product. Registration embeds on the school site.

9. Donations

One-time and recurring give, optional round-up, same family wallet.

Development is not a third processor with a third family list.

10. Accounting + QuickBooks

The school keeps QuickBooks as books of record. AnnaSIS posts operations.

We do not replace the accountant’s system. We stop the office from typing the same family three times.

11. Parent uniform swap

School-facilitated exchange beside the store.

Used uniforms stay in the community without a Facebook thread the office cannot see.

12. Workflow engine

Serial or parallel approvals. Packaged for athletics clearance; reusable for enrollment packets and purchase approval.

Clearance, packets, and custom processes are configured by the school — not another vendor.
