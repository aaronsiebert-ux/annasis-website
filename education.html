Education pillar

Twelve capabilities. One family record.

Sell modules first if you must. The suite is what you show when the office is tired of FACTS + FinalForms + Eventbrite + a store + QuickBooks in five places.

1. Admissions / enrollment

Inquiry → application → decision → contract → re-enroll. Embed Apply on the school’s existing website. No second marketing site required.

2. SIS

Demographics, households, attendance, scheduling, staff roles. System of record for the office — not a gradebook that later grows a finance surprise.

3. Grades

Gradebook, report cards, transcripts, GPA. Teachers live here; families see the same record in a responsive portal.

4. Tuition

Plans, invoices, incidental fees, family-portal pay. Finance is in the $44 card — not a module that triples the bill.

5. Health

Immunizations and health fields on the student. Forms that expire (physicals) reuse the same workflow engine as athletics.

6. School store

Catalog and cart for uniforms, books, and fees. Institutional commerce, not a volunteer Shopify.

7. Communication

Email + SMS to groups, teams, and lists. Responsive parent portal. No required store app.

8. Events

Camps, enrichment, games, fundraisers — register and pay without Eventbrite leakage.

9. Donations

One-time and recurring give, optional round-up, same family wallet as tuition and store.

10. Accounting + QuickBooks

School keeps QBO as books of record. AnnaSIS posts operations. Different from FACTS gravity and Veracross native GL.

11. Parent uniform swap

School-facilitated exchange beside the store — a faith-school motion most SIS tools ignore.

12. Workflow engine

Serial or parallel approvals. Packaged for athletics clearance (physicals, packets, AD/trainer/coach queues). Reusable for enrollment packets and purchase approval.
