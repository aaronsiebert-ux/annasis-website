Who we serve · Churches

One-stop for churches — event management, giving, check-in, and store.

ANNASIS helps churches run the ministry year in one place: event management for conferences, large events, and multi-session programs; online giving and fundraising; check-in for kids and volunteers; and a real store for merch and resources. Planning, check-in, giving, fundraising, store & events — church language first, built for how offices actually serve families.

What you get

A one-stop shop for church operations that matter every week and every season.

Household and people records for programs sit under the same roof as registration, giving, check-in, and commerce — so families are not bounced between disconnected tools for every ministry signup.

Online giving & fundraising

Own giving as a core pillar: online donations, campaign gifts, and fundraising that share the same institutional footing as your programs and store — so generosity and registration are not scattered across separate tools. Sponsor invites let families, staff, and others invite sponsors for events, participants, and similar attendees. Round-up on church store purchases drives additional giving at checkout.

Event management — conferences & programs

Event management for conferences, large gatherings, and multi-session programs — plus life groups, camps, VBS, classes, sports leagues, midweek, galas, and guest events. Register and ticket the full ministry calendar with paid and free signups, payments, and the details offices collect — without a consumer ticketing site.

Check-in for kids, families & volunteers

Check-in for programs and Sunday-adjacent ministry — kids and families, volunteers, and the rooms that need a clear in/out path. Tied to the same people and program records your office already uses for registration.

Store & e-commerce

Merch, resource tables, books, apparel, and program materials on an institutional store — not a volunteer side shop. Useful for church bookstores, event add-ons, and shared school–church communities. Optional round-up at checkout turns store purchases into extra giving.

School + church overlap

Many private schools have churches. Many churches visit the school site.

Christian and church-affiliated schools already live in ANNASIS’s primary market. Families, camps, athletics, and ministry events often cross the hallway between sanctuary and classroom. ANNASIS is a school operating system first — churches use the same giving, events, check-in, and store capabilities when that overlap is real.

When this lands

Signals you may be a fit.

Office reality

  • Giving, fundraisers, and ticketed nights live in separate tools from program registration.
  • Conferences, life groups, VBS, camps, and sports each have their own signup stack.
  • Check-in for kids or volunteers is disconnected from the programs you just registered.
  • Resource tables and merch are a separate Shopify or cash box.
  • A church and school share families — and parents feel every extra login.

How we talk about it

Church language for the church office; private-education adjacent language where a school is in the same community. Visible brand is ANNASIS. Modules when you are ready — jog before you run. Talk with sales about which programs, giving paths, and check-in to bring online first. For partnership culture, roadmap, and how we work across segments, see How we partner.
