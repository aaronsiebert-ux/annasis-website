What you get

Church, school, camp and store on one family record.

Most church tools stop at the church. School, camp and store end up in separate systems with separate family lists. ANNASIS keeps one household across all of them.

Online giving & fundraising

Online giving and fundraising campaigns sit next to event payments, so a family gives and registers in the same place.

  • One-time and recurring giving, offered at checkout.
  • Round-up to one or more funds, including on store purchases.
  • Goal meters and leaderboards for campaigns and events.
  • Sponsor invites for events and participants.

Fundraising comes with the optional Tuition, Billing & Fundraising Power-up.

When this lands
