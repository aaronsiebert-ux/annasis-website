What ANNASIS is for churches

A modular path for event registration, store commerce, and fundraising. Designed so offices can turn on the programs that hurt first — then expand. Same ANNASIS voice as schools: one community record where it helps, without forcing every pillar live on day one.

What we are not claiming

ANNASIS is not positioned as a full Planning Center–class replacement for worship planning, media and music, volunteer service scheduling depth, or deep pastoral care CRM. Keep the ChMS that runs Sunday. Bring ANNASIS where registration matrices, merch, and multi-program commerce need more than a simple signup form.

School + church overlap

Many private schools have churches. Many churches visit the school site.

Christian and church-affiliated schools already live in ANNASIS’s primary market. Families, camps, athletics, and ministry events often cross the hallway between sanctuary and classroom. ANNASIS is built as a school operating system first — churches use the same event, store, and fundraising modules when that overlap is real.

Church-affiliated schools

If you operate (or partner with) a private school, start on the school operating system. Events, store, donations, athletics clearance, and family portal share one foundation with admissions, grades, and tuition when you are ready.
