Are we a fit?

Does this sound like your office?

Private schools have customers — Parents, Students, and Faculty — in a way public schools do not. Schools often need separate tools and processes because their current system does not cover the full operation — event registration, forms, a volunteer store, QuickBooks retyping beside the SIS. AnnaSIS has the foundation to address those operations on one student and family record. If three or more lines below are true, start with events and the store. Keep your SIS until the office is ready.

Pain checklist

Pains for Parents, Students, Faculty — and the office

Events still live outside the SIS

Parents still pay athletics fees, camps, and fundraisers on Eventbrite or forms while tuition sits in the student system.

AnnaSIS: Parents register and pay on the school record — sports, plays, camps, fundraisers — without add-ons and high fees.

Athletics clearance is a separate product

Students and Faculty still chase physicals, waivers, and coach clearance in FinalForms or paper — disconnected from fees and the family record.

AnnaSIS: Athletics + workflows puts clearance on the same household as roster and fees, so Students and Faculty are not juggling another login.

The store is a second login

Parents buy uniforms, books, and trips in Shopify or a volunteer shop that never matches the tuition household.

AnnaSIS: Parent purchases land beside tuition — easier for families, cleaner for the office.

Uniform exchange is a Facebook thread

Parents trade used uniforms in Facebook groups Faculty and the office cannot see or support.

AnnaSIS: Parents publish, exchange, or purchase in the portal — no middleman, visible to the school.

“Cheap SIS” until finance shows up

The gradebook looked affordable until tuition, messaging, or admissions became paid add-ons.

AnnaSIS: Tuition and finance are part of the operating system — not a later SKU.

QuickBooks is right — the typing is wrong

Someone retypes the same family into the SIS, the store, and the books.

AnnaSIS: Keep QuickBooks as books of record. Operations post so the office stops triple-entry.

You are not ready to rip out the SIS this year

The board will not fund a full platform cutover, but registration and commerce still hurt.

AnnaSIS: Start with events and the store beside your current SIS. Expand modules when the office is ready.
