Who we serve · Store

Institutional store & ecommerce — for schools, camps, churches, and event programs.

ANNASIS helps offices sell what the community needs without a volunteer side shop: uniforms, books, merch, and fees for schools; camp store gear and session add-ons; church resource tables and apparel; and event add-ons beside registration. Optional round-up at checkout drives additional giving — and store paths sit beside sponsor invites and donations when you fundraise for students, campers, attendees, or events.

What you get

Commerce on the same institutional footing as registration and giving.

Household and people records sit under the same roof as the store, fees, and checkout — so families are not bounced to a separate Shopify login for every purchase.

Store catalog & checkout

Uniforms, books, trips, merch, apparel, gear lists, and program materials on an institutional store — not a volunteer side shop the office reconciles at night. Paid fees and simple catalogs for busy seasons.

Purchases beside people records

Store purchases land on the same household or attendee footing as tuition, registration, or program fees — cleaner for families and for the office. No second shop login to reconcile.

Round-up & sponsor adjacency

Optional round-up at checkout turns everyday purchases into extra giving. Store paths sit beside sponsor invites and donations when your campus or program fundraises for students, campers, events, or similar participants.
