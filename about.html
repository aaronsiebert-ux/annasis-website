Empowering Student, Parent, Faculty, and School success

Designed for private schools — where families and the school matter.

ANNASIS started when our founder was asked by a private school, where his children attended, to build the system they actually needed: households, tuition, events, a store, and the office’s QuickBooks — software that serves Parents, Students, and Faculty as customers, not just an internal roster.

Our mission: Empowering Student, Parent, Faculty, and School success. We are a trusted partner whose software helps private schools run well so they can spend the hours on Parents, Students, and Faculty — not on five logins. Unlike public schools, private schools have customers; those experiences matter. The result is a better experience for Parents and Students, streamlined tools and operations for the office, and lower cost for private schools.

School in a box

The public name is ANNASIS. Not just a student information system but a school operating system — Empowering Student, Parent, Faculty, and School success. The twelve modules are the foundation of that system — not an all-inclusive catalog. Event registration and the school store sit on that base.

Who we serve first

Private K–12 schools first — where Parents, Students, and Faculty are customers of the campus experience. Camps and related programs that share those families may come later. They do not set the homepage.
