About us

Designed with a Christian school — not adapted from a district.

AnnaSIS started when our founder was asked by a Christian school, where his children attended, to build the system they actually needed. The product is written for private and faith-based operations: households, tuition, events, a store, and the office’s QuickBooks.

Our mission is to be a trusted partner whose software helps education and ministry institutions run well so they can spend the hours on students and the Gospel — not on five logins.
