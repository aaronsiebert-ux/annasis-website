About us

Designed for private schools — not adapted from a district.

AnnaSIS started when our founder was asked by a private school, where his children attended, to build the system they actually needed: households, tuition, events, a store, and the office’s QuickBooks.

Our mission is to be a trusted partner whose software helps education institutions run well so they can spend the hours on students and the Gospel — not on five logins.

School in a box

The public name is AnnaSIS. The category is a school operating system. Events and the store are pillars of that system.

Who we serve first

Private K–12 schools. Camps and related programs that share those families may come later. They do not set the homepage.
