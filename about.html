Empowering Student, Parent, Faculty, and School success

Designed for private schools — where families and the school matter.

As parents and customers of private education, we started ANNASIS to help private schools succeed. This began with understanding the real challenges private schools face — high costs, complex processes, disjointed systems, and a limited budget. The solution was to create a “school-in-a-box.” Admissions, SIS, Gradebook, tuition & billing, health & medical records, school store, communication, event registration, donations & fundraising, QuickBooks accounting, uniform exchange, and approvals on a common platform. Software that serves Parents, Students, and Faculty as customers, not just an internal roster.

Our mission: Empowering Student, Parent, Faculty, and School success. We are a trusted partner whose software helps private schools run well so they can spend the hours on Parents, Students, and Faculty. Unlike public schools, private schools have customers; those experiences matter. The result is a better experience for Parents and Students, streamlined tools and operations for the office, and lower cost for private schools.

School in a box

The solution is ANNASIS. Not just a student information system but a school operating system — Empowering Families and the School’s success. The foundation of that system — Admissions, SIS, Gradebook, tuition & billing, health records, school store, events, donations & fundraising, uniform exchange, sports/approval processes, and a communication platform. Where most SIS platforms fall short — and where ANNASIS is built to be different — is features like ecommerce, events management, approvals, and uniform exchange without separate systems. Some peers cover one or two of those jobs; few cover all of them without supplemental systems.

Who we serve first

Built for K–12 private educational institutions that value the student, parent, and faculty experience. We understand that private schools are a business and have customers. An all-in-one school operating system for the whole school day: inquiry, apply, enroll, re-enroll, attendance, schedules, households, grades, report cards, transcripts, GPA, tuition, incidental fees, cafeteria, field trips, book store, uniforms, spirit wear, used-uniform swap, health forms, immunizations, physicals, athletics clearance, sports tickets, seasons, tryouts, games, plays, concerts, camps, enrichment, fundraisers, donations, round-up, email, SMS, parent portal, approvals, purchase requests, and QuickBooks — without a second login for the office or the family.
