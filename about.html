Designed with and for our customers

Built in partnership — not handed down from a catalog.

ANNASIS started with private schools, where Parents, Students, and Faculty are customers. From that foundation we partner the same way with event and conference offices, institutional stores, camps, and churches: listen first, adapt the platform to the vision of each organization, and ship what the office actually needs to run well.

Our mission stays the same across every community we serve: Empowering Student, Parent, Faculty, and School success — and the families and volunteers those offices serve. We are a long-term partner, not a bolt-on vendor. The result is software shaped with customers, clearer experiences for the people they serve, and simpler operations for the office.

Who we serve

Schools — and the other pillars beside them.

Private schools remain the heart of the school operating system. The same partnership and configurable platform also power events, the store, camps, and churches — so related offices do not need a different vendor for every season.

One platform, adapted to your vision

For schools, ANNASIS is a school operating system — not just a student information system. Admissions, SIS, Learning Management & Gradebook, tuition & billing, health records, the school store, events, donations & fundraising, uniform exchange, athletics eligibility, and communication sit on one footing. Where most SIS platforms fall short — ecommerce, event management, approvals, and uniform exchange without separate systems — is where we tailor with the campus. Some peers cover one or two of those jobs; few cover them together, and fewer still reshape the product around a school’s specific vision.

Five communities. One partner.

Schools run the day — enrollment through athletics — with Parents, Students, and Faculty as customers. Events offices run registration, check-in, exhibitors, and fundraising for conferences and calendars. Store teams sell uniforms, spirit wear, and fees without a second stack. Camps manage sessions, attendance, and giving. Churches run programs, check-in, and giving beside the same institutional footing. Across all five, we design with the office — not for a generic buyer.

How we work

Partnership is how the product gets built.

We sit with leadership and the people who run the office, learn the vision for the campus or program, and adapt ANNASIS to that vision — modules, workflows, and roadmap priorities in the open. That is how a private school’s SIS becomes their operating system, and how event, store, camp, and church teams get software that matches how they already serve families.
