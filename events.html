Who we serve · Events

Configurable event registration for complex programs, conferences, and calendars.

ANNASIS helps offices run the calendar in one place: registration and ticketing for school events, camps, church programs, conferences, and conventions; custom workflows for the forms, clearances, and approval steps your organization actually uses; check-in and scheduling for busy days; honest support for exhibitors where your event needs them; and fundraising with sponsor invites and round-up beside payments — not in a separate stack.

What you get

Event operations that matter every season — not a consumer ticketing side trip.

People and household records sit under the same roof as registration, payments, check-in, and giving — so families and attendees are not bounced between disconnected tools for every signup.

Registration & ticketing

Online registration and ticketing for paid and free events — school sports and plays, camp sessions, church programs, conferences, and convention-style gatherings. Capacity, payments, and the details offices collect without a consumer Eventbrite-style stack.

Check-in & scheduling

Check-in paths and program scheduling tied to the same people and event records you use for registration — so door lists, sessions, and room-level detail stay on one foundation. Depth for multi-session days: map with sales to what you actually run.

Exhibitors & event add-ons

Where your event honestly needs booth or exhibitor signup, resource tables, or paid add-ons, keep those paths beside registration — not a second vendor spreadsheet. Useful for conferences and conventions; talk with sales about the depth your show requires.

When this lands

Signals you may be a fit.

Office reality

  • Registration and ticketing live in a consumer tool while people records live elsewhere.
  • Check-in and door lists do not share records with the events you just sold.
  • Conferences, camps, school nights, and fundraisers each have their own signup stack.
  • Sponsorship and giving are disconnected from registration and checkout.
  • Exhibitors or add-ons are tracked in a spreadsheet beside the ticketing site.
