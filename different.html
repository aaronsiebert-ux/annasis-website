What makes us different

A school operating system — not another login.

Private schools serve Parents, Students, and Faculty as customers — not just an attendance roll. Peers still leave tickets, clearance, or a store outside the household. AnnaSIS was written so those jobs stay on the same record, at the same rate. The result is a better experience for Parents and Students, streamlined tools and operations for the office, and lower cost for private schools.

Designed for private schools — where families and the school matter

The founder was asked by a private school to build what the office actually needed. That origin still governs the product: families, tuition, events, store, and QuickBooks — written for private education, not adapted from a public-school SIS.

One rate. No hidden or add-on finance costs.

The operating system is one offer — not a cheap gradebook that becomes expensive when tuition, messaging, or admissions show up as add-ons.

A single parent portal for an exceptional experience

One family portal — apply, register, pay, and stay informed on the school’s own site. Parents get a clearer campus experience without a second brand or a consumer ticketing destination.

Honest first site

If the office will not move its current student system this cycle, AnnaSIS can sit beside it and run the modules that fill the gaps — event registration, school store, athletics eligibility, communication, billing, and more. That is a scoped first site of the same product — not a disconnected add-on company.
