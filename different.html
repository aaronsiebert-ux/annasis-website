What makes us different

A school operating system — not another login.

Private schools serve Parents, Students, and Faculty as customers — not just an attendance roll. Peers still leave event registration, ecommerce, athletics, forms and approvals, the school store, or uniform exchange outside the SIS — some cover a few of those jobs; few cover all of them. ANNASIS was written so those jobs stay on the same system, at the same price. The result is a better experience for Parents and Students, streamlined tools and operations for the office, and lower cost for private schools.

Designed for private schools — where families and the school matter

The founder was asked by a private school to build what the office actually needed. That origin still governs the product: Admissions, SIS, Gradebook, tuition & billing, health & medical records, school store, communication, event registration, donations & fundraising, QuickBooks accounting, uniform exchange, and athletics eligibility & workflows — written for private education, not adapted from a public-school SIS. Where most SIS platforms fall short — and where ANNASIS is built to be different — is putting event registration, school ecommerce, athletics and sports, forms and approval workflows, the school store, and uniform exchange in the same system. Some peers cover one or two of those jobs; few cover all of them without a bolt-on stack.

One price. No hidden or add-on finance costs.

The operating system is one offer — not a cheap gradebook that becomes expensive when tuition, messaging, or admissions show up as add-ons.

A single parent portal for an exceptional experience

One family portal — apply, register, pay, and stay informed on the school’s own site. Parents get a clearer campus experience without a second brand or a consumer ticketing destination.

When you are ready

If the office is unable to replace the system this cycle, ANNASIS can sit beside it and run the modules that fill the gaps — event registration, school store, athletics eligibility, forms, communication, billing, and more. Jog before you run.
