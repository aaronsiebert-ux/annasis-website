What makes us different

A school operating system — not another login.

Private schools serve Parents, Students, and Faculty as customers — not just an attendance roll. Peers still leave tickets, clearance, or a store outside the household. AnnaSIS was written so those jobs stay on the same record, at the same rate.

Designed with the school, not adapted from a district

The founder was asked by a private school to build what the office actually needed. That origin still governs the product: households, tuition, events, store, and QuickBooks — not a public-district SIS with faith language added later.

One rate. No Lite tax.

Small schools should not lose finance, events, or clearance because those sit in a higher tier. The operating system is one offer — not a Lite package that grows expensive when money and approvals come online.

Embed on the site Parents already use

Apply, register, and pay can live on the school’s own pages. We do not require a second brand or a consumer ticketing destination.

Honest first site

If the office will not move its current student system this cycle, AnnaSIS can sit beside it and run the modules that fill the gaps — event registration, school store, athletics eligibility, communication, billing, and more. That is a scoped first site of the same product — not a disconnected add-on company.
