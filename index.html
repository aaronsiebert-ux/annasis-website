The system

A foundation for how a school office runs.

Twelve foundation modules — not an all-inclusive list — including Admissions, student information, Attendance, Scheduler, Behavior / Discipline, Gradebook and progress reports, tuition & billing with payment plans, health & medical records, the school store, email and SMS, event registration, donations & fundraising, QuickBooks, uniform exchange, analytics and reporting, and athletics eligibility workflows with waivers and consents.
