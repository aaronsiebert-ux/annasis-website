Who we serve · Camps

One-stop for camps — registration, camp store, attendance & fundraising.

ANNASIS helps camps run the season in one place: online registration for day, overnight, and multi-session programs; a real camp store for merch and materials; attendance and check-in paths; and fundraising that sits beside your programs — not in a separate tool. Registration, staff & medical forms, camp store, attendance & fundraising — camp language first, built for how directors and offices actually serve families.

What you get

A one-stop shop for camp operations that matter every summer and every session.

Household and camper records for programs sit under the same roof as registration, store, attendance, and fundraising — so families are not bounced between disconnected tools for every session signup.

Registration & program signups

Online registration for day camps, overnight sessions, auxiliary programs, and multi-week summers — paid and free paths, capacity, waivers where you need them, and the details offices collect. Built from the same event registration depth schools and churches already use for busy calendars.

See Events →

Camp store & e-commerce

Merch, apparel, gear lists, and program materials on an institutional camp store — not a volunteer side shop or a separate Shopify. Useful for summer retail, session add-ons, and shared school–church–camp communities. Optional round-up at checkout turns store purchases into extra giving for the season.

See Store →

Attendance & check-in

Attendance and check-in paths tied to the same people and program records you use for registration — so day-camp mornings and session rosters stay on one foundation. Authorized pickup and room-level detail: talk with sales about the depth your program needs.

Fundraising & campaign gifts

Fundraising and campaign gifts that share institutional footing with your programs and store — scholarships, season campaigns, and community asks without scattering generosity across a separate stack. Sponsor invites let families, staff, and others invite sponsors for campers and similar attendees. Round-up on camp store purchases drives additional giving at checkout. Giving adjacency when your camp sits beside a school or church community.

Forms, health info & program ops

Collect the forms and health information parents must provide before camp — allergies, emergency contacts, waivers, and session paperwork — on the same registration path. We phrase this as health-info collection and program ops, not a camp nurse EHR or cabin-assignment suite. Staff paperwork and season ops: map with sales to what you actually run.

Multi-program summers

One community path when you run more than one session type — day + overnight, sports weeks, enrichment, family camp weekends, and retreat-style events — without a new consumer signup stack for every week.
